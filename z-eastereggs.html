🏏 The Sachin post — r/ThirdUmpire

Post timestamp "Nov 5, 2009" - the exact day Sachin Tendulkar scored his legendary 175 against Australia (Hyderabad, 2009).
Sidebar "175k members" - his score in that innings: 175.
Sidebar "Created Nov 14, 1992" - the date of cricket's first-ever third-umpire decision, and Sachin was the first batsman in history given out by one (run out, Durban).
